Pocket FFM launch access model

approved

Cole approved distribution-gated open registration with email verification and accepted limited referral leakage outside FFM.

Evidence

  • A QR code will be shown to approximately 1,000 pre-vetted FFM attendees.
  • Anyone with the shared link may register free after proving email ownership.
  • No member roster, CSV allowlist, password, or model call is required during registration.
  • The beta-code path remains available behind an environment flag.
  • Load testing must cover 1,000 landing hits and at least 300 registrations inside 3 minutes before steady answer traffic.

Limitations

Distribution is intended for the FFM community, but strict membership enforcement and minor referral leakage are intentionally out of scope for launch.
