G2 hybrid retrieval design

pass

A production-safe hybrid retrieval design was completed from verified current architecture and baseline evidence.

Evidence

  • The current production path uses the content chunk corpus through lexical fallback, so that corpus is the canonical source of truth.
  • The design specifies one-to-one lexical and vector shadow indexes keyed by canonical chunk identifier, preventing cross-corpus double counting.
  • The rollout uses reciprocal-rank fusion, preserved citations, post-retrieval answerability grading, honest no-answer behavior, and unanswered-question capture.
  • Evaluation gates cover retrieval quality, answerability, citations, unsupported claims, latency, and rollback readiness.
  • The application test suite passed 10 of 10 after the design and reviewer artifacts were created.

Limitations

This is a design receipt only. No production retrieval change was implemented. Embedding provenance and extension compatibility must be proven in staging before implementation.
