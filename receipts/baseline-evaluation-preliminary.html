A fixed 25-question baseline was run against current read-only production retrieval, and a reviewer packet plus structured scoresheet are ready.

Evidence

  • Answerability decision matched the expected behavior for 16 of 25 questions.
  • Citation rule satisfied for 25 of 25 questions.
  • Mean retrieval latency was 208.31 milliseconds and P95 was 771.87 milliseconds.
  • Largest gaps appeared in agent attraction, paid lead-source comparison, CRM specifics, open-house greetings, and follow-up scripts.

  • No production question records were created and the production service remained healthy.

Limitations

Auto-scores are preliminary. FFM subject-matter review and independent verification are required before gate approval.
